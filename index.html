Welcome to the Baseball Guide

Baseball is a popular sport that uses a bat and ball that is played between two opposing teams.

The objective of the game is to score more runs than the opponent

The two teams take turns playing offense by hitting the ball and defense by fielding the ball.

Game Statistics Summary

Statistic Abbreviation What It Measures
Batting Average AVG Abatter's hits divided by their at-bats
Home Runs HR Times a batter hits the ball out of the park
Runs Batted In RBI Runs that score as a result of a batter's action
Earned Run Average ERA The average number of earned runs a pitcher allows per nine innings
