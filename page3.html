Leagues and Teams

Major League Baseball is split directly into two main organization groups.

These groups are known specifically as the National League and the American League.

Each league is further split into three geographic divisions: East, Central, and West.

Famous Historic Franchises

  • New York Yankees (American League East)
  • Los Angeles Dodgers (National League West)
  • Chicago Cubs (National League Central)
