Essential Rules of Baseball

A standard game of baseball is played across nine distinct segments called innings.

During each inning, both teams get a chance to bat and try to score runs while the other fields.

The batting team keeps hitting until the fielding team records three outs to switch sides.

Ways to Record an Out

  • Strikeout: The pitcher throws three strikes to the batter.
  • Flyout: A fielder catches a batted ball in the air before it hits the ground.
  • Tag Out: A defensive player tags a runner with the ball while they are off base.
